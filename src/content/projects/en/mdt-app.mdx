## The brief

In the FTTH rollout at MD Telekom many threads come together: invoicing, site
documentation and coordination between office and field technicians. The MDT App
brings all of that into one mobile-friendly application.

## What I delivered

- Web/mobile app for invoicing preparation and site documentation
- Real-time planning and coordination between office and technicians
- Optimized for on-site use (mobile-first)

## Takeaways

Software for the field lives and dies by real-world use: stand once with a
technician on a construction site, and you understand why every tap counts.

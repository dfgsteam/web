## Background & Requirements

During the FTTH NE4 fibre rollout, daily planning, quantity surveying and site documentation had to be handled through various manual processes and fragmented media. Information was lost or arrived late.

**Core requirements:**

- Central communication and documentation interface between field engineers and the office
- Capture measurements on-site directly via smartphone
- Document dynamic build progress per building and residential unit
- Automatically generate exportable documentation

## Solution & Implementation

Built with **Flask (Python)**, **JavaScript** and **jQuery**, served via **Gunicorn** behind **Nginx** on a self-hosted Unix system.

**Functional highlights:**

- **Mobile app usage** by field engineers directly on site
- **Daily planning** for residential unit rollout in the FTTH NE4 area
- **On-site quantity surveying** during the build
- **Dynamic build progress view** within buildings – down to individual apartment level
- **Real-time interface** between site and office

**Documentation & export:**

- Complete site documentation prepared automatically
- Export to **PDF** and **Excel**
- Document preparation for subsequent postal dispatch

**Reporting:**

- Analysis functions across time periods and individual buildings or projects

> "From apartment to whole building – rollout, documentation and reporting in one application."

## Result

The app replaces paper-based workflows with a seamless digital process. Field engineers document on-site via smartphone while the office accesses all data in real time.

Measurements, build statuses and documentation are captured consistently, exported automatically and available for analysis at any time – resulting in greater transparency, fewer errors and significantly more efficient project management during the FTTH NE4 rollout.

## Background & Requirements

Creating exposés, profitability calculations and grid pre-inquiries for photovoltaic projects previously required significant administrative effort. Multiple separate tools and manual steps led to wasted time and fragmented data.

**Goal:** drastically reduce office work, automate processes as far as possible – everything from a single system.

## Solution & Implementation

Built with **Flask (Python)**, **JavaScript** and **jQuery**, hosted on own **Nginx** infrastructure.

**Functional highlights:**

- **PV Exposé Generator** – professional client documents produced at the click of a button
- **Grid pre-inquiries** – structured support and preparation for submissions to the grid operator
- **Profitability & amortisation calculations** – transparent figures for sales and planning
- **Peak-shaving analysis** – optimisation potential surfaced directly from the system
- **Project cost estimation** – fast, straightforward project costing

**System properties:**

- Login and user management
- Intuitive interface with clearly structured process steps
- Central hub for all project information in one place

> "Less office, more project: photovoltaic processes – digital and lean."

## Result

The application significantly cuts administrative overhead: exposés, calculations and inquiries are generated automatically and managed centrally.

Manual paperwork is minimised, turnaround times shortened and document quality standardised. Planning, sales and technical assessment of PV projects all run efficiently from a single system – instead of scattered spreadsheets and individual documents.

## The idea

ennery is energy consulting software in the cleantech space: tools that speed
up viability checks, client exposés and technical analyses while preparing
them in an understandable way.

## What I build

- Calculation and analysis backend with Python & FastAPI
- Clear UI for consultants
- Export and reporting functions
- Scalable, containerised infrastructure

## Why it works

Energy consulting lives from numbers that convince. The software consolidates
the data basis, calculates transparently and delivers results that can be used
directly in the client conversation – less Excel, more speed.

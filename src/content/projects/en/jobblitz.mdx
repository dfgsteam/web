## Background & Requirements

The "Jobblitz" platform is in live operation and continuously expanded with new functionality. The goal is stable maintenance, introduction of new features for existing and new product lines, and integration of additional systems and interfaces.

At the same time, the existing codebase needed to be progressively modernised and improved in quality – without disrupting ongoing operations.

## Solution & Implementation

Built on **Symfony** as the primary framework, served via **Nginx**.

**Key activities:**

- Continuous maintenance and bug fixing in the live system
- Implementation of new features and modules for new products
- Integration of external services and APIs
- Integration of **CleverPush** as a push notification system
- Revision and extension of frontend components and designs
- Continuous refactoring and modernisation of the existing codebase

**Working approach:** close coordination with the development team, iterative delivery within the existing codebase, with attention to performance, usability, maintainability and scalability.

> "Evolution, not standstill – continuous development of a live platform."

## Result

The platform is kept technically current, its feature set and product offering progressively expanded, and user experience along with system stability sustainably improved. The existing codebase is being structurally modernised and made more maintainable for the long term.

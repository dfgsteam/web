## The brief

Preparing grid connection applications means: lots of data, lots of touchpoints,
lots of paper. EN.MIE needed a central application that combines site
documentation and the preparation of connection requests in a single workflow.

## What I delivered

- Custom web app for capturing and maintaining all site data
- Structured workflow for preparing grid connection applications
- Clear roles and permissions for client and team

## Takeaways

To map processes in software you first have to really understand them. Most of
the work went into modelling the real-world workflows – after that, the rest was
almost routine.

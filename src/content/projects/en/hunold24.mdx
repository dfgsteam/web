## The idea

hunold24.de stands for bespoke software: applications that are tailored
exactly to a company's process – no compromises, no gaps between tool and
everyday work.

## What I build

- Custom web apps with PHP & Python
- Workflow and process automation
- Database and backend architectures
- Deployment included (Docker, CI/CD, hosting)

## Why it works

Standard software forces processes into predefined shapes. Custom apps work
the other way around: they map the process as it actually runs – and grow with
the business as it changes.

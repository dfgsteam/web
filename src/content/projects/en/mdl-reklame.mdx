## The brief

Complaints happen. The question is how you handle them. MD Landmaschinen needed
a system that captures, tracks and evaluates every complaint – instead of
letting them get lost in the email inbox.

## What I delivered

- Complaint management system with a clear workflow
- Capture, status tracking and history for each case
- Reports as the foundation for service improvements

## Takeaways

Complaints are priceless feedback. Capture them systematically and you turn
phone-call frustration into concrete product improvements.

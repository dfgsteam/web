## Background & Requirements

Complaints were previously captured across various channels and tracked manually – leading to delays, a lack of transparency and fragmented information flows.

**Goal:** a unified complaint management system with clearly defined process steps, visible responsibilities and the guarantee that no case falls through the cracks. All relevant information centrally available and clearly presented.

## Solution & Implementation

Built with **Flask (Python)** and **JavaScript**, hosted on own **Nginx** infrastructure.

**Functional highlights:**

- **Automated complaint workflows** with fixed, defined process chains
- **Guided case handling** – structured and traceable step by step
- **Central information hub** – all data in one place
- **Visual status overview** – current state, history and next steps at a glance
- **Intelligent reminder system** – automatic follow-ups to prevent unresolved cases
- **Alternative decision paths** – all possible handling routes mapped per case type

> "Every complaint in view – clearly structured, automated and on schedule."

## Result

Complaint processes were standardised, accelerated and made transparent for all parties involved. Open cases no longer get stuck: automatic reminders and clearly defined workflows actively drive resolution forward.

All data is held centrally in one place, communication is simplified and every individual complaint is traceable in a structured and audit-ready way.

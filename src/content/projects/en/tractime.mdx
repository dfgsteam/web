## Background & Requirements

Trello has no native, practical time tracking for individual tickets and boards. External tools are often cumbersome to connect or lack the required feature set.

**Goal:** time tracking directly within the Trello interface – simple to use for both team members and project managers, with transparent reporting – without leaving the familiar working environment.

## Solution & Implementation

Built with **Symfony (PHP)**, containerised via **Docker**, deeply integrated through the **Trello API**.

**Functional highlights:**

- Time tracking directly in Trello tickets – no context switching
- Quick, simple booking of working hours with a click
- Reporting by ticket, board and time period
- Support for standard working-time recording scenarios

**Technical implementation:**

- API integration with Trello for seamless embedding
- Server-side processing via Symfony
- Docker containerisation for flexible deployments and easy hosting

> "Track time where it happens – right inside Trello."

## Result

Tractime provides an integrated time tracking system for Trello. Time can be logged directly per ticket, evaluated and used for working-time documentation. Teams get transparent reporting at board and task level – without leaving their usual workflow.

## The brief

If you work with Trello, you don't want to constantly switch between the board
and a separate time tracker. Tractime brings time tracking directly into Trello –
no context switching.

## What I delivered

- Trello app for time tracking right on the board
- Clean integration with the Trello API and solid data handling
- Reporting of tracked time per card and list

## Takeaways

Great tools disappear into the workflow. Tractime showed how important it is to
place tracking exactly where the work actually happens.

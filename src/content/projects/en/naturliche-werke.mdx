## The brief

Natürliche Werke wanted more than just an online shop – the brand needed a web
presence that credibly communicates naturalness and quality.

## What I delivered

- Concept and web design of the complete presence
- WooCommerce shop with a clear product structure
- Consistent implementation of the brand identity in color, type and imagery

## Takeaways

Brand projects live from dialogue: the earlier the content is in, the better the
result. Design without a content strategy stays a pretty shell.

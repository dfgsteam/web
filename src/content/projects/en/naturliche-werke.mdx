## Background & Requirements

"Natürliche Werke" had no digital presence at all. The goal was to create a modern, stripped-back and visually strong website that presents the handcrafted work in an emotional and clear way.

Three core requirements drove the project:

- **Authenticity** – the site should make the personality and craftsmanship behind the brand tangible
- **Natural aesthetic** – calm, organic forms instead of cluttered layouts
- **Intuitive navigation** – clear visual language and minimal structure for an immediate impression

## Solution & Implementation

Built on a fully custom theme using **HTML, CSS and JavaScript** – no CMS, no page builder, just bespoke code for maximum creative control.

**Approach:**

1. Consulting on structure, visual impact and design directions
2. Development of multiple layout variants for selection
3. Final refinement into a consistent brand identity
4. Technical implementation and launch
5. Temporary hosting via own infrastructure

> "Clear design, natural visual language – the website conveys exactly what the work stands for."

## Result

The outcome is a modern, consistent and image-focused online presence that communicates the "Natürliche Werke" brand clearly and presents the person behind the business effectively.

Visitors get an immediate visual impression of the work – without distraction, without noise.

## The problem on the construction site

An FTTH rollout is fundamentally a logistics problem: many technicians, many
construction sites, many steps between office and field. Without software,
invoicing data gets lost in spreadsheets, site documentation wanders around on
paper, and planning becomes a guessing game.

## What the app needs to deliver

The MDT App grew out of this practice – and it showed what mobile field support
really needs:

- **Offline-capable:** there is not signal everywhere on site
- **Mobile-first:** every tap has to work with gloves or wet fingers
- **One place for everything:** invoicing preparation, site documentation and
  planning in one application instead of three tools

## The lesson for client work

To digitise a process, you first have to understand reality. I spent days
accompanying technicians and asking where the work really comes to a halt.
That is where the value comes from – not from the technology, but from
understanding the process. That applies to every industry.

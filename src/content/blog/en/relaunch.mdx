## Finally online again

The site has been completely rebuilt – as a static portfolio with Astro, GSAP
and Tailwind. No CMS, no backend: content lives as Markdown directly in the
repo, versioning included.

## Why static?

- **Speed:** Only what is actually displayed gets delivered.
- **Security:** There simply is no server to attack.
- **Maintainability:** A new project is a Markdown file, nothing more.

## What is new?

- Horizontal scrolling through the projects (desktop) with a vertical fallback
- German & English, with a clean language switcher
- Dark mode
- Complete legal pages (imprint & privacy policy)

Enjoy browsing!

## A shop is more than a theme

Most WooCommerce projects fail not at the plugin but at the structure. Before I
design a single pixel, I clarify three questions:

- **Product model:** what is a product really – simple, variable or a bundle?
- **Order flow:** what does the path from order to delivery look like?
- **Inventory logic:** what happens on stock-out, returns, pre-orders?

## What makes the difference in practice

Managing shops for Amazon and WooCommerce taught me that data quality beats
everything. A consistent naming scheme, clean attributes and sensible
categories pay off in every later step – from filtering to shipping to
complaints.

## Order automation as a lever

The biggest effects come from systematising complaints and order status.
Instead of getting lost in the inbox, every case is captured, tracked and
evaluable. Reported problems turn into concrete improvements – that transforms
support frustration into product quality.
